# G1 evaluator validation status

G1 is open. G0 closed the portable trace and evaluator interface. Five completed categorical motif calibrations cover fixed synthetic populations and observation budgets. The Stage 1 gate requires evidence across fixture classes, representations, scales, and estimator dimensions before freezing a profile. [G0 closeout](G0-closeout.md)
