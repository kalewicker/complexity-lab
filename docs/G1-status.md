# G1 evaluator validation status

G1 is open. G0 closed the portable trace and evaluator interface. Four completed categorical motif panels cover fixed synthetic populations and observation budgets. The Stage 1 gate requires evidence across fixture classes, representations, scales, and estimator dimensions before freezing a profile. [G0 closeout](G0-closeout.md)

| Requirement | Current evidence | Remaining work and pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| E01a development fixtures and structural scoring | Tier-0 reference fixtures and categorical held-out code accounting. The matched repetition and growth panel failed its registered criterion with 20 of 24 pairs passing against the required 22. | Diagnose and validate support for incidental short motifs under a fresh protocol, extend to the planned fixtures and three scales, and validate the supported structural estimand alongside the other profile dimensions. [Tier-0 fixtures](E01-tier0-fixtures.md) · [Repetition and growth results](E01-categorical-repetition-growth.md) |
| E01b representation and transport invariance | Tier-0 equivalence under relabeling and interleaved optional chunks. | Check critical F11, F12, F19 and F23 invariants for the profile being claimed. Require exact common-code equality on small equivalent fixtures and the plan's scale tolerance or declared sampling interval for approximate measures. [Fixture results](E01-tier0-fixtures.md) |
| E01c null and sensitivity calibration | Four motif panels passed for their declared categorical populations. Each used 350 independent worlds per stratum. The 99 permutations inside each world set the rank resolution. | Cover the remaining critical IID, configuration and temporal nulls applicable to the profile. Use independently generated worlds for binomial bounds and prespecify a simultaneous error allocation across critical strata. Pass the registered false-positive upper and sensitivity lower bounds at declared effect sizes and budgets. [Anchored results](E01-categorical-motif-dictionary-anchored-calibration.md) |
| E01d blind evaluation | The current evidence is from engineering fixtures and disclosed synthetic generators. | Lock the profile and fixture draws across three scales. Meet at least 90% of prespecified pairwise orderings and all critical invariants. Record individual failures and require fresh blind fixtures after a repair. |
| E01e ablations and cost | Categorical comparators and finite evaluator budgets are implemented. | Run the fixed-subset ablations for dictionary, context depth, quantization, and sample budget. Report detection limits and computation costs. |
| G1 metric profile | Tier-0 registry and provenance are implemented. | Publish `metric-profile-v1.json` with estimator versions, nulls, scales, thresholds, failure cases, and the fixture report. If some dimensions fail, freeze the validated dimensions as an explicit partial profile and restrict claims to its scope. [Registry results](E01-registry-results.md) |
